Kinetic AI Exercise Guide

Perfect Squat Form

  • Camera Framing: Stand side-profile or 45-degree angle to camera for knee depth tracking.
  • Depth: Lower hips until thighs are parallel to ground (knee angle < 95°).
  • Common Errors: Excessive torso lean forward, knees buckling inward, heels lifting off floor.

Perfect Push-Up Form

  • Camera Framing: Position camera side-on at floor level to capture shoulder-elbow line.
  • Depth: Lower chest until elbows reach 90° or lower.
  • Common Errors: Hips sagging or piking, elbows flaring out sideways.

Perfect Hammer Curl Form

  • Camera Framing: Stand front-facing or 3/4 angle with arms clearly visible.
  • Execution: Curl dumbbells toward shoulders with palms facing each other.
  • Common Errors: Swinging torso momentum, elbow drift.

Perfect Lunge Form

  • Camera Framing: Stand side-profile with full body visible from head to toe.
  • Execution: Step forward and bend back knee toward floor to 90°.
  • Common Errors: Front knee extending past toes, torso leaning forward.

Perfect Overhead Press Form

  • Camera Framing: Stand front-facing so both arms and shoulders are visible.
  • Execution: Press weights overhead until arms lock out directly above head.
  • Common Errors: Arching lower back, uneven/asymmetrical arm drive.
